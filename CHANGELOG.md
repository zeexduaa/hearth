# Changelog

## Unreleased (0.1.0)

First version.

- Recurrences: daily to yearly, every N units, a day of the month (month ends handled), the nth
  or last weekday, and a month of the year.
- Money kept exactly in whole cents per currency, typed as 12.50, $1,234.56, 30 EUR or Rs 2500.
- Inventory: locations, prices, purchase details, serial numbers, warranties (by date or
  length), tags, and items marked as gone.
- Supplies with minimums and a shopping list.
- Upkeep and chores: fixed or after-done timing, costs, skipping, undo, and turns among
  household members.
- Bills: due dates, payments with actual amounts, overdue tracking, autopay, end dates,
  spending per month, a monthly estimate, and alerts for unusual amounts.
- Receipts and manuals kept in a de-duplicated folder, with an integrity check.
- A home inventory for insurance (terminal, printable HTML, CSV) and CSV exports.
- A command line with a dashboard of what needs attention.
